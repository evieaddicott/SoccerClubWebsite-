About Us

Our mission at Lamorinda is to provide open opportunities for players to reach the next level. We have a strong alumni base of college soccer players at schools such as Stanford, UNC, and UCLA. The Girls program competes in the Girls Academy League and the Boys compete in MLS-next. Lamorinda is a club that plays collective soccer, focusing on forward positioning with a purpose to score goals. We are a club that is focused on player development and creating a positive environment for players to grow and develop as players and people.
